## How Much Do Online Medical Weight Loss Programs Cost?

Online medical weight loss costs depend on which services a program includes. You may pay separate fees for clinician visits, a membership, lab work, and medication. Compare the full set of costs, not just the price featured in an ad.

A clinician visit, program membership, and prescription may each carry a separate charge. ### Treat introductory and “starting at” rates as estimates

Promotional offers and “starting at” prices may not reflect what you’ll pay over time. For instance, Walgreens says eligible self-pay patients may pay as little as $149 per month for select products and doses. The price can vary by medication and dose, and Walgreens notes that listed prices may differ from its usual charges.

### Check whether prices cover one month or ongoing care

A monthly price may apply only to a membership or prescription. Visits, lab work, and follow-up care could cost extra. Some programs also require a longer commitment, even when they display a monthly rate.

Before enrolling, confirm how often you’ll be billed, whether there’s a minimum term, and how cancellation works. Ask whether follow-up appointments, refills, or dose changes come with additional fees. It’s also worth finding out what you may owe if a clinician decides a medication isn’t appropriate for you. A provider should explain the costs and care plan clearly, without promising a prescription before an evaluation.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How much should I budget for an online medical weight loss program?**\
Costs vary by provider and care plan. Your total may include clinician visits, membership or coaching, lab work, medication, pharmacy fees, and delivery. Ask for an itemized estimate and compare costs over several months, not just the first month.

**Does a program’s monthly fee usually include medication?**\
Not always. Some fees cover access to clinicians or coaching, while prescriptions and pharmacy services are billed separately. Confirm what the quoted price includes, and ask whether medication costs depend on the product or dose.

**Will insurance cover online weight loss care or GLP-1 medication?**\
Coverage depends on your insurance plan, the service, and the medication’s intended use. Ask your insurer about benefits, copays, exclusions, and prior authorization requirements. Check visits, lab work, and prescriptions separately.

**Will an online consultation guarantee a prescription?**\
No. A licensed clinician must review your health information and decide whether medication is appropriate. Ask what you may owe for the evaluation if the clinician recommends a different approach.
